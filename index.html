How We Work

🧭

Human Agency

People define purpose, constraints, acceptance criteria, and authority. Consequential actions require explicit authorization.

🔬

Evidence Before Claims

We publish methods, reproducible benchmarks, negative controls, known limitations, and corrections when results do not hold.

🌐

Open Interoperability

We prefer composable interfaces, public protocols, local-first options, and standards that let others inspect and build on the work.

Research, prototypes, pilots, and production software are labeled separately. A passing unit test is not a security audit, customer validation, or production-readiness claim.

Build With Us

We welcome technical contributors, research collaborators, design partners, customer discovery conversations, and supporters of open infrastructure.
